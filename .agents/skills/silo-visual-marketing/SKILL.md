# Silo Visual Marketing

Produce marketing visuals with accurate Silo products, believable human use and creative direction suited to the brief. Use this shared context to select sources, write precise instructions, inspect results and correct defects.

## Work from the brief and originals

Identify the requested artifact, scene/action, audience, format and permitted changes. Discover available source context before asking about a missing choice. Use the requested tool and medium. Footage repair remains a video task when supplied stills are only detail references.

Inspect source pixels before describing appearance and relevant footage through time before describing motion. Documentation establishes product behavior; real product references establish visible construction and contact. Generated layouts can establish required inventory and placement, but cannot establish physical geometry. Keep identity, style, layout, action and first/end-frame roles explicit.

State the allowed change and protected features in the actual tool instruction. Translate Silo-specific names into source-anchored descriptions of appearance, location and relationships using [physical fidelity](references/physical-fidelity.md); the generator does not inherit this skill's context. Carry those descriptions into the submitted prompt and reference assignments, including after optimization or shortening. Preserve product shape, transparent walls, channel/rocker relationships, control glyphs, screen/light states and existing correct regions. Creative lighting, camera, food, wardrobe and effects follow the brief; they do not redefine the hardware or its operation.

## Review, correct and deliver

Compare results with the originals at composition and detail scale, including regions meant to remain unchanged. Check each relevant product feature yourself before handing back a finished result, even for food-only or background-only edits. For video, use the full temporal, audio and edit checks in the video workflow. A successful tool response or appealing composition does not establish fidelity.

Use a bounded repair for an isolated defect when correct regions can be retained. Regenerate from stronger originals when construction or multiple relationships are wrong, within the user's authorized generation scope. Reinspect the complete result after each correction; a repaired button does not establish an intact ring, screen, channel or lid. Do not claim a final result preserves the product while known defects or critical visible details remain unverified. Disclose inspection limits and remaining defects; keep the result labeled as a candidate when it cannot be verified or corrected.

Deliver the requested file or paste-ready prompt. Verify the exported file opens and its measured dimensions, duration and format match the brief. Keep project naming and source dependencies intact. Distinguish an accepted direction, a usable component and an accepted final deliverable.
