## Identify the right file

Start with subject/action, campaign, required format/crop and editability. List the relevant folder or search concise title terms, including spelling variants. Narrow by parent, date, duration, aspect ratio and preview content. Campaign names can be absent from filenames; an empty title search does not establish that the asset is absent.

Check canonical file ID/link, parent, MIME, size and available dimensions/duration before downloading. Same-name folders and different-ID documents remain distinct until contents establish their relationship. When a document has been edited, confirm the intended copy by title, owner, modification date and ID rather than assuming the original link contains the changes.

Shortlist previews and inspect actual pixels or temporal content for product visibility and task fit. Opaque camera filenames need visual inspection or a bounded contact sheet. Retrieve the relevant original for editing; a preview, flattened image or lone project may not supply the required layers, resolution or media.

Use established spelling variants for container/conteiner, blueberry/bluberry, testimonial/testemoniel and bundle/bundels; include crop/subtitle terms such as noBG, mobile, desktop, nosub, 9_16 and 4_5 when relevant. Filename labels are search clues, not proof of aspect ratio, transparency or content.
