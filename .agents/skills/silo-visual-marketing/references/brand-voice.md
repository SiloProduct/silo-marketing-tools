# Brand voice

Use for captions, scripts, narration and marketing copy. Silent imagery does not need copy guidance. The canonical voice source is [The Silo Brand](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BVO06vBGu80aK7XjYa92fZ1T-ljXKV3N/view?usp=drivesdk), BRAND VOICE & LANGUAGE.

## Persona and tone

Silo speaks as a down-to-earth, whip-smart kitchen assistant: helpful, efficient, delightful and purposeful. Write inviting, conversational, refined and direct copy. Give useful information simply, get to the point and put the customer at the center of the story.

Use natural speech, concrete viewer relevance and a purposeful opening. Avoid inflated language, stacked fragments, generic technology promises and copy that merely repeats discussion notes. Use restrained energy and occasional wit that fits the moment; keep the product and intended action clear.

## Vocabulary

Favor ordinary language about food, keeping, kitchens and connected usefulness over technical or inflated category language. Explain the container-wall airway plainly when it matters. Documentation may also call it an air tunnel, air port or air channel; retain the source terminology when interpreting a technical description. Do not change locked copy solely to impose a synonym.

Keep reviewer preferences and campaign wording in the brief. Apply punctuation, narrator tone, hook structure, food/seasonality and CTA to the intended audience and production rather than treating a previous campaign's choices as permanent brand rules.

## Scripts for visual work

Preserve the agreed concept and narrator perspective through revisions. For a product hero, orient unfamiliar viewers to the category and relate captions to visible features. Align comparison copy with its pictured comparison and supported basis. Place specifications in graphics or speech according to the brief and viewer comprehension.

A first-person device narrator can be a marketing character. Keep that character's voice distinct from actual interaction prompts and user replies. Actual device speech and display messages require matching interaction guidance or approved audio; marketing tone does not establish shipped utterances.

For modular clips, assembled dialogue and generated narration, use [video workflow](video-workflow.md).

## Claims

Voice principles, historical copy and artwork do not validate performance figures, savings, freshness durations, safety claims, offers or depicted behavior. Use a current source relevant to the brief and preserve exact wording, units, population and timeframe alongside quantitative claims in the working script.

Resolve conflicting figures or permissions before using the affected line. Do not turn averages into guarantees, freshness estimates into safety certifications or internal positioning into a customer-facing tagline. A claim accepted in one production does not establish approval for every campaign.
