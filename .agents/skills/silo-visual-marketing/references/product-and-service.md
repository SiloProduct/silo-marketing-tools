# Product and service context

Use when depicting Silo, its containers, food storage or the relationship to the app. [Physical fidelity](physical-fidelity.md) covers visible construction and reference selection.

## Product anatomy and service

Silo is a countertop vacuum-storage base used with Silo containers. Air is extracted through the container's bottom connection; the container has a wall-side airway/air tunnel connecting the upper air space to that connection. Do not depict food being drawn through an exposed bottom hole, a hose plugged into the lid or a pump mounted on top of the container.

Distinguish the container-wall airway, the lid vacuum indicator and the base display/light ring. The lid indicator can become fully blue when vacuumed; neither the entire lid nor the base ring is that gauge. Exact indicator movement and internal routing need matching diagrams or footage.

Connected food management links container identity, vacuum events and measured contents to an app inventory entry. Labeling gives the food a digital name; it does not print or attach a physical label. The app helps track freshness estimates, storage time and consumption. Freshness guidance depends on initial condition and handling and is not a food-safety certification.

## Everyday interaction

| Stage | Correct depiction |
|---|---|
| Prepare/place | Put food in a Silo container, seat its separate lid and place the bottom flat centrally on the base glass. |
| Start vacuum | Apply downward pressure on the closed lid. There is no separate vacuum-start lid button; placement alone, a screen tap and rocker release are not substitutes. |
| Run/wait | Withdraw hands after initiation. The closed container remains on the base through vacuuming and the following contents check/weighing. |
| Label when needed | Wait for “Pick up to label,” lift the container, then respond to the spoken contents question. Store after the required interaction. |
| Open/use/reseal | Press or lift the rocker on its own hinge to open the air valve and release the vacuum. Then lift off the separate main lid. After partial use, replace the lid and reseal on the base. |

The rocker is hinged to its own assembly; the main lid is not hinged to the container body. A complete tutorial must preserve contact, action order and required waits, with exact spoken/display states matched to the intended experience.

## Labeling, resealing and basic mode

Contents need labeling when an existing label is absent or new contents are detected. This is not restricted to a physically unused container. Existing labeled food after partial consumption can retain its entry while the system updates its weight; do not append another naming exchange to every reseal.

Basic mode retains vacuum and scale while omitting connected labeling, inventory and freshness features. Choose the mode and content state represented by the scene before adding app interactions or dialogue.

## Other depicted actions

- The front screen is a display, not a touchscreen. The base has physical controls, and container pressure initiates vacuuming.
- Weighing ingredients is a scale interaction distinct from vacuum initiation; use matching footage for its controls and timing.
- To stow the empty base, remove the container and raise the front of the whole appliance into upright orientation. It does not open like a clamshell. Use real footage for human grip and cord movement.
- Specify container arrangements as nesting inside another container or placing closed containers one on top of another. Do not infer interlocking or stability features.
- Match food suitability, storage claims, voice provider, app UI and screen/light behavior to current guidance relevant to the brief.
